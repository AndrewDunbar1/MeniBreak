# 3. Model recipes

These recipes define the internal comparisons in the MeniBreak document. They
are distinct from the frozen `mbpr-derivation-v1` scoring artifact.

## Dynamic top-eight validation engine

- Horizons: 3, 5, and 7 years, with the fixed endpoint rule in `START_HERE.md`.
- Outer validation: 5 repetitions of patient-grouped 5-fold splitting.
- Selector: chi-square ranking inside each outer training fold; retain top 8.
- Estimator: logistic elastic net, `l1_ratio = 0.25`, fixed `C = 0.1`, with
  predictors standardized from training-fold statistics.
- No candidate screening, selection, scaling, or fitting may see the outer test fold.
- WHO and integrated grade are direct ordinal discrimination scores, not
  penalized regression fits.

The executable specification is the Python workflow, not the separate R
`lambda.1se` model-development scripts.

The old Word report's six AUCs are not the benchmark for this executable
implementation. Fresh results and the forensic reconciliation are recorded in
`docs/09_INTERNAL_REPRODUCTION_AUDIT.md`.

## Dynamic top-eight alone

At each horizon, select eight binary 5%-of-arm breakpoint-presence windows inside
each training fold. The fitted feature identities may vary by fold.

## Clinical plus dynamic top-eight

Five clinical variables plus the eight fold-selected indicators: age in years, `log1p(mitoses)`,
gross-total resection (`gtr`), prior radiotherapy (`prior_rt`), and adjuvant
radiotherapy (`adj_rt`). Binary variables use 0/1.

This is 13 inputs per fitted fold, matching the recovered source report.

## Scale-free breakpoint geometry

The strongest freshly reproduced breakpoint-only analysis uses 56 fixed shape
features derived from arm-relative breakpoint distributions. They summarize
normalized radial position, dispersion, concentration, entropy, loss/gain route
asymmetry, and related shape statistics without using clinical variables or
thresholded arm-level SCNA calls.

Validation uses ten repetitions of patient-grouped five-fold outer validation.
Within each outer training set, a binomial `cv.glmnet` model uses `alpha=0.25`,
patient-grouped inner folds, standardized predictors, and `lambda.1se`. Test
patients do not influence penalty selection or fitting. The exact feature and
validation lineage is in `archive/method-development/analysis/03_geometry_route_bakeoff.R`
and `04_scale_free_geometry_multihorizon.R`.

## Clinical plus SCNA

The same five clinical variables plus the 39 signed arm calls (`-1` loss, `0`
unaltered, `+1` gain) generated by the specified arm-coverage threshold. The
threshold and preprocessing lineage must be stated with every result.

## Clinical plus SCNA plus breakpoint architecture

The clinical-plus-SCNA predictors plus 18 breakpoint features:

`total_breakpoint_boundaries`, `breakpoint_arms_touched`,
`breakpoint_route_entropy`, `max_arm_breakpoint_fraction`,
`telomeric_breakpoint_fraction`, `central_breakpoint_fraction`,
`centromeric_breakpoint_fraction`, `mean_rel_tel_to_cent`,
`sd_rel_tel_to_cent`, `cna_segment_count`, `loss_segment_count`,
`gain_segment_count`, `median_segment_length`, `small_segment_count_lt5mb`,
`dynamic_hotspot_breakpoints_all`, `dynamic_hotspot_breakpoints_non1p`,
`dynamic_hotspot_arms_used_all`, and `dynamic_hotspot_arms_used_non1p`.

The direct lineage script is
[`archive/method-development/analysis/08_clinical_integrated_models.R`](../archive/method-development/analysis/08_clinical_integrated_models.R).

## Locked SEG-only software model

`mbpr-derivation-v1` has no clinical inputs. It freezes 44 SCNA-derived inputs
(39 signed arm calls, 3 burden summaries, 1p loss, and 22q loss) plus the 18
breakpoint features above. Coefficients, preprocessing, scaling, imputation,
calibration, seeds, and reference percentiles are stored in its checked artifact.
See [`MODEL_CARD.md`](../MODEL_CARD.md).
