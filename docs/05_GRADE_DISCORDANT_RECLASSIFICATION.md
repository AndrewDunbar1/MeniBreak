# 5. Grade-discordant reclassification

This stage asks whether breakpoint-informed cross-validated risk separates
tumors whose outcomes disagree with grade: elevated grade (WHO or prespecified
integrated grade 2/3) without recurrence by the horizon, and WHO grade 1 with
recurrence by the horizon.

Only out-of-fold predictions may be used. The risk threshold must be chosen
without using the evaluated tumor and stated explicitly. Every denominator must
be recalculated from recorded grade, recorded outcomes, and the horizon rule;
manual slide labels are not authoritative.

The analysis is descriptive reclassification. It does not establish a treatment
threshold, sensitivity/specificity operating point, or clinical decision rule.
