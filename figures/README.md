# Figures

Publication figures for the breakpoint panel. Every panel is drawn from the CSVs
in [`../results/reference/`](../results/reference) — the same values indexed in
`RESULTS_MANIFEST.yaml`. Each figure ships as `.png` here; `.pdf` versions were
generated by the same scripts.

Throughout: **Broad** = the full institutional cohort; **Restricted** = primary
tumour, gross-total resection, no prior/adjuvant/induced radiotherapy,
non-angiomatous. All AUCs are patient-grouped out-of-fold.

| Figure | Shows | Source |
|---|---|---|
| `figure1_discrimination.png` | Discrimination of WHO grade, integrated grade, arm-level SCNA and the 8-window panel at 3/5/7 years, in both cohorts. The panel matches integrated grade at 5 years and exceeds it at 7. | `full_ladder.csv` |
| `figure1b_discrimination_with_combined.png` | As Figure 1, with the combined `SCNA + panel` model added, showing the incremental gain over SCNA alone. | `full_ladder.csv` |
| `figure2_specificity.png` | Specificity at ~80% sensitivity. WHO and integrated grade are **0.00** at every horizon in both cohorts — a three-level ordinal cannot be thresholded to 80% sensitivity without calling nearly everything high risk. The continuous panel score reaches 0.45–0.62. | `clinical_metrics_operating.csv` |
| `figure3_scna_plus_breakpoints.png` | Arm-level SCNA versus SCNA + the panel, by horizon and cohort — the incremental-value test. | `clinical_metrics_discrimination.csv` |
| `figure4_elevated_grade.png` | Among tumours that **never recurred despite elevated grade** (the patients grading would over-treat), the fraction correctly called low risk. Against integrated grade the panel rescues 16/20/25% at 3/5/7 years where arm-level SCNA rescues only 4/5/10%. Higher is better; all models thresholded at ~80% sensitivity. | `clinical_metrics_elevated_grade.csv` |
| `figure5_discordance_broad.png` | Reclassification of grade-discordant tumours in the broad cohort — both directions (grade over-called and under-called). | `grade_discordance.csv` |
| `figure5_discordance_restricted.png` | The same reclassification analysis in the restricted cohort. | `grade_discordance.csv` |
| `figure6_full_ladder.png` | The complete model ladder, from single-label grade through the 13-, 60- and 102-feature models, in both cohorts. | `full_ladder.csv` |
| `figure7_parsimony.png` | AUC against **number of features in the model** (1 → 8 → 13 → 60 → 102). The parsimony curve: 8 binary windows reach integrated-grade performance, and 13 features (panel + 5 clinical covariates) capture most of what 102 features achieve. | `full_ladder.csv` |

## Regenerating

These were produced by the manuscript figure scripts in the source project
(`07_manuscript/code/make_nejm_figures.py` and companions), which read the same
result CSVs shipped in `../results/reference/`. Those plotting scripts are not
included here; the figures are shipped as artefacts, and the underlying numbers
are fully available in the CSVs and the manifest.

**Caveat.** These figures show internally validated, single-institution results.
Read them with [`../docs/LIMITATIONS.md`](../docs/LIMITATIONS.md).
