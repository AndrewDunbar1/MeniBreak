The MeniBreak report numbers were **not** produced in a ChatGPT online thread.
They were produced by a local analysis repository, and the code and outputs have
been recovered and are shipped in this repository.

## Why the "ChatGPT workspace" claim was rejected

An earlier provenance note asserted the scripts existed only in a ChatGPT
workspace and that the report "preserves the originating conversation URL"
`https://chatgpt.com/c/6a73c967-…`. This was checked directly and does not hold:

- Neither `MeniBreak.docx` nor `breakpoint_panel_word_tables_fixed.docx` contains
  any `chatgpt`/`openai` URL, the conversation id, or any tool mention — not in
  `docProps/core.xml`, `app.xml`, `custom.xml`, the relationship files, or the
  raw document bytes.
- The report's custom properties carry only an `abstract` and the date
  "4 August 2026"; its application field is Microsoft Word.
- The scripts and CSVs are physically present on the drive (above), so "never
  downloaded" is false.

The URL was therefore not evidence from the files. Provenance is the local
`BreakpointsFoundationModel` repository.

## Verification performed

- Every value in `RESULTS_MANIFEST.yaml` is read from the shipped CSV and matches
  `MeniBreak.docx` to the printed precision (e.g. broad-cohort ladder
  0.702/0.735/0.752, 0.836, 0.850, 0.844/0.857/0.849; comparators carry an
  explicit `locked_published` column of 0.69/0.766/…).

  executed end-to-end on the synthetic cohort and recovers the planted chr1p
  window.
